1 · Reference & Lookups

Code-first reference tables shared by every cycle, member and ledger row: ethnic/region demographics, savings-product families, pot lifecycle states and mobile-money/bank payout channels.

2 · Groups & Members — the chama chapters

Village savings associations / saving circles (a "chama"). Carries the chapter, its members and officers, and the governance entities of the fuller union model (meetings, membership links).

3 · Chamas — rotating pots & merry-go-round

The rotating saving rounds (tontine / esusu / equb): one pot per cycle, who is collecting, and the round-robin / table-banking payouts that decide who draws the lump sum.

4 · SACCOs — savings & credit

Savings & credit cooperative wing: contributions/(cash-in), solidarity loans out of the group float, loan repayments, meetings and member credit scoring history.

5 · Financial & Banking

Money plumbing: payout channels (Orange Money · MTN · Moov · Airtel · bank · cash), the polymorphic financial ledger, annual/tax returns and admin audit logs.
